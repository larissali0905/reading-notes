### 三、同类错误句子
1. **时态错误**：
    - I eat an apple last night.
    - They go to the cinema two days ago.
2. **冗余错误**：
    - The color of the red car is bright.
    - The size of the big box is large.
3. **被动语态问题**：
    - The door is openeded by him.
    - The letter is sended by her.

### 四、错误原因分析
1. **时态错误**：
    - 正确应该是 I ate an apple last night. 和 They went to the cinema two days ago.，原句中描述过去发生的动作却使用了一般现在时，应改为一般过去时。
2. **冗余错误**：
    - 应改为 The color of the car is bright. 和 The size of the box is large.，原句中“red”与“color”、“big”与“size”语义重复。
3. **被动语态问题**：
    - 正确应该是 The door is opened by him. 和 The letter is sent by her.，“open”和“send”的过去分词形式分别为“opened”和“sent”，原句形式错误。
